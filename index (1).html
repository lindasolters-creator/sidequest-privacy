SideQuest Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use the SideQuest mobile application and related services.

1. About SideQuest

SideQuest is a real-world quest and discovery application designed to help users discover interesting activities, challenges, experiences, and campaigns.

We use information to provide the SideQuest experience, including helping users discover nearby quests, track quest progress, verify quest completion, and provide XP and in-app rewards.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as your display name, username, account identifier, authentication information, and other information necessary to maintain your account.

Location Information

If you grant location permission, we may use your device's location to identify nearby quests and determine whether you are within the required distance of a quest location.

We do not currently publicly display your precise current location to other users.

Quest and Activity Information

We may collect information about quests you discover, accept, start, complete, or otherwise interact with.

This may include quest progress, completion history, XP, rewards, and related campaign information.

Photos and Proof Submissions

Some quests may require you to submit a photograph as proof of completion.

Photos submitted for verification may be transmitted to our servers and processed for the purpose of determining whether the submitted proof satisfies the requirements of the quest.

We do not currently publicly display submitted proof photos to other users unless we introduce a feature that specifically provides for such sharing.

Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information necessary to operate, secure, maintain, and improve SideQuest, such as device type, operating system, app version, network information, diagnostic information, and technical logs.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Provide and operate SideQuest features.
  • Show quests that may be relevant or nearby.
  • Determine proximity to quest locations.
  • Track quest progress and completion.
  • Process submitted proof photos.
  • Perform automated quest verification.
  • Provide XP and in-app rewards.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve the performance and reliability of SideQuest.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. AI and Automated Verification

SideQuest may use automated technologies, including artificial intelligence systems, to assist with verifying photo proof submitted for certain quests.

When you submit a proof photo, the image and relevant quest information may be processed by our verification systems to determine whether the submitted evidence appears to satisfy the requirements of the quest.

Important: Automated verification is used as part of the SideQuest quest completion process. We do not use submitted proof photos to publicly identify your precise location.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or provide access to information with service providers that help us operate SideQuest.

These may include providers supporting:

  • Cloud hosting.
  • Database services.
  • Application infrastructure.
  • AI-powered verification.
  • Security and fraud prevention.
  • Technical diagnostics and analytics.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal processes, enforce our policies, protect our rights, or protect the safety and security of SideQuest and its users.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Information transmitted between the SideQuest application and our online services may be protected using encryption in transit, such as HTTPS/TLS, where supported by the relevant service.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SideQuest services, maintain accounts, provide quest history and rewards, maintain security, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your SideQuest account and associated personal information.

Account deletion requests may be submitted using the account deletion method provided by SideQuest.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required or permitted by law to retain certain information.

Important: Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

9. Children's Privacy

SideQuest is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.

10. Location Permissions

SideQuest may request access to your device's location.

Location information is used primarily to provide location-based features, including identifying nearby quests and determining whether you are close enough to a quest location to participate.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. If you deny location permission, certain location-based features may not function correctly.

11. Third-Party Services

SideQuest may rely on third-party service providers to operate certain parts of our service, including hosting, databases, infrastructure, security, analytics, and automated verification.

These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review the applicable policies of third-party services when appropriate.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SideQuest, our practices, legal requirements, or applicable services.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

These rights may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.
