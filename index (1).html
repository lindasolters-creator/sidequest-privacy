About SideQuest

SideQuest ("SideQuest," "we," "us," or "our") is an Android app that turns everyday surroundings into real-world quests. We help users discover nearby quests, complete them, and earn XP and in-app rewards for doing so.

SideQuest is not a government, financial, health, news, or educational app. It is a general-audience activity and discovery app intended for teenagers and adults.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the SideQuest app (package name com.anexis.sidequest), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the categories of information described below. We only collect what we need to run SideQuest's core features: account creation, quest discovery, quest completion, and rewards.

Account information

When you create a SideQuest account, we collect information such as your username, email address, password (stored in encrypted form), and any profile details you choose to add.

Location information

SideQuest can use your device's location to find quests near you and to check your proximity to a quest location when you attempt to complete it. We do not publicly display a user's precise current location to other users.

Quest/activity information

We store information about the quests you view, start, and complete, along with related progress data such as XP earned, rewards unlocked, streaks, and completion history.

Photos and proof submissions

When a quest requires photo proof, we collect the photo you submit. Submitted photos are sent to our servers so that the quest can be verified.

Device and technical information

We collect standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and general diagnostic data that helps us keep SideQuest working correctly.

03

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your SideQuest account.
  • Show you quests that are nearby or relevant to you.
  • Determine your proximity to a quest location so the app can confirm you're in the right place.
  • Verify photo proof submitted for quest completion.
  • Track and store your quest progress, XP, and in-app rewards.
  • Load and update quests and campaigns from our online services.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app, including diagnosing crashes and technical issues.
  • Communicate with you about your account or your quest activity when necessary.
04

AI and Automated Verification

When you submit a photo as proof of completing a quest, that photo may be sent to our servers for automated AI verification. Our AI verification system reviews the submitted photo to determine whether it satisfies the specific requirements of that quest.

This automated process is used to support quest completion and reward decisions. Photos submitted for verification are handled as described in this policy, including the sections on Data Security and Data Retention below.

05

How We Share Information

SideQuest does not currently sell users' personal information.

We may share information with service providers who help us operate SideQuest, such as hosting our servers, storing data, and running the automated verification systems described above. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function for us.

We may also share information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of SideQuest, our users, or the public.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect the information we collect from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. This includes measures such as encrypted storage of account credentials and access controls on our systems.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

07

Data Retention

We retain account information, quest and progress data, and photo submissions for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide SideQuest's features to you.

If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

10

Location Permissions

SideQuest requests location permission so it can show you quests near your current area and check whether you're close enough to a quest location to complete it. You can grant, deny, or revoke this permission at any time through your device's system settings.

If location access is denied or revoked, some features of SideQuest — such as finding nearby quests or confirming proximity for completion — may not work as intended.

11

Third-Party Services

SideQuest relies on online services to load and update quests, campaigns, and app content, and to support features such as photo storage and AI verification. These service providers process data on our behalf and are only given the information necessary to provide their service to SideQuest.

This policy covers SideQuest's own data practices. If a third-party service linked from within the app has its own privacy policy, that policy governs how that third party handles your information.
